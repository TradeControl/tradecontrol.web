# VAT production-credentials legal submission documents

This folder contains the public pre-launch Privacy Statement and managed-hosting Terms and Conditions supplied as supporting material for Trade Control's HMRC VAT production-credentials application.

## Status

These are submission drafts, not effective customer-facing legal documents. Trade Control's `tradecontrol.co.uk` deployment is the sandbox and accreditation environment used to prove the intended multi-tenant topology. The commercial product is not live and production filing authority is not enabled.

The documents describe the intended accredited service while retaining explicit publication gates for unfinished operational, security, commercial and legal matters. They must not be linked from the public product website as effective policies until those gates are complete and the final text has received legal approval.

- [Managed Hosting Terms and Conditions submission draft](trade-control-terms-and-conditions-submission-draft.md)

## Provenance

Both documents were derived on 26 October 2026 from the corresponding legal drafts in Trade Control's private internal hosting repository and reconciled against the completed public accreditation evidence on 30 October 2026. The public `tradecontrol.web` revision containing these documents is the controlling submission source that HMRC can open. Private repository paths and revisions are retained only in the internal evidence record.

This repository holds the submission copies so a public, revision-specific URL can identify exactly what accompanied the application without suggesting that the drafts are already effective on the Trade Control website.

Before submission, replace any remaining open publication-gate language only where retained evidence and legal review support a definitive statement. Do not invent service commitments, retention periods, subprocessors, accreditation status or an effective date.
