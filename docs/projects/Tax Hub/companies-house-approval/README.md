# Companies House approval evidence

This folder contains the public, non-secret evidence package supporting Trade Control's request for approval of its initial Companies House XML filing scope:

> **Unaudited filleted FRS 105 micro-entity accounts.**

## Evidence package

- [Test-submission evidence dossier](companies-house-test-submission-evidence.md) — the four-case programme, submission identifiers, gateway evidence, mathematical checks and current approval status.
- [STD cash statement — Years 1 and 2 baseline](STD-cash-statement-years-1-and-2-baseline.xlsx) — the pre-transition cash statement used to reconcile the first-accounts and genuine-comparatives cases.
- [STD cash statement — Year 3 additive transition](STD-cash-statement-year-3-additive-transition.xlsx) — the post-transition cash statement used to reconcile the richer Year 3 case.

The two workbooks are retained unchanged from the versions used for the recorded reconciliations. They show the accounting history behind the statutory figures; the evidence dossier explains how that history was validated through the Equity Bridge, comparative continuity and the Companies House test gateway.

Presenter credentials, company authentication values, complete credentialed GovTalk envelopes and Companies House correspondence are excluded. Those remain protected external evidence.
